# Introduction
The purpose of this post is to document how I'm feeling before, during and after an aggressive minicut. I have done a minicut before and it worked extremely well for me. 4 weeks of extreme calorie reduction is better for my mental state compared to 8-16 weeks of a slow consistent diet. After completing the minicut ending at 65kg, I bulked back up to 70kg and am now starting to max out machines at my gym and my shoulders are now able to ripple akin to how I can ripple the individual muscle fibres in my chest (I am now showing a video sorry). Interestingly, during my last minicut, my resting heart rate went from 60bpm to about 40bpm which suggests that when I lose fat my heart doesn't need to pump as much blood so I may in the future reduce how much I bulk up before I cut. 


# Workout plan during minicut
I will continue to do an upper lower split however, I will start reducing the amount of sets per exercise including warmup sets. 

## Upper day
Bench press: 1 warmup set 1 top set no backoff sets
Lat pulldown: 1 top set no warm up with 0 rir 
Row (any variation): 1 warmup set followed by 1 topset with 2 RIR
Bicep: 1 top set 0 rir
Front Delt: 1 top set 0 rir 
Tricep: 2 top steps 1 rir 
Side delts: 1 top set 0 rir 

All top sets are to be done at 5-8 reps. 

## Lower
Hacksquat: 2 warmup sets with 1 top set at 2 RIR 
Leg extension: 1 top set with 0 RIR
Hamstring curls: 1 top set with 0 RIR 
Abs/Abductors: Skip or 1 top set with 2 RIR depending on how I feel.

## Cardio
Incline treadmill for probably an hour every other day depending on how I feel. 


# Diet 
TDEE = 2200 calories (without cardio so I expect it to be higher during my cut due to the incline treadmill)
Beef mince + Potatoes or Tuna and rice. Liver / Beef heart 1x week. I will eat this every day for 4 weeks with variation. Realistaically I just need to hit 84g protein and 45g of fats. I can play around with carbs depending on how I feel so anything between 130-155g carbs. Multi vitamin + electrolytes in the gym will hopefully help with mental state. 
I take Psyllium Husk for my stomach and I expect this to really help with satiety. My last minicut, I was having beef + rice and while that was so incredibly tasty, the satiety of rice isn't the best so I'm going to experiement with potato.

This will set my calorie deficit to about -1000 which is going to have a dramatic effect on my hormones.


# Joural 
## Before Cut 
13/09/2026 I feel pretty aight. My hormones and mental state are all fine although at the end of my cut it's worth exploring why I put a lot of my self worth on my physique and aesthetics. I didn't ask a girl I liked out because I didn't want her to see me shirtless and now I'll never see her again. 

## During Cut

15/09/2026. 2 days into the cut and I'm hella hungry. I've noticed that I have crazy food noise during the afternoon and evening. When I am hungry all I can think about is food and eating something, anything. In about a week or so it'll go away but it's quite fascinating to see and acknowledge that I have food noise because I never thought I had it. 

Mentally, I'm still pretty fine. I am noticing a decline in my mental processing speed and it's taking a while for me to even write this blog. Interestingly, meditation is becoming slightly easier. I meditate before bed where I alternate between anchors. Normally my anchor is my breath but other times it'll be sound, gratitude (thinking about things I'm grateful for), mantras etc. I think it's because I'm so tired I don't have the mental energy to think. 

Doing 1 set for my sets at the gym seems to be working fine although I am noticing already I don't have the energy to do my sets. I'll come back in a week or so. 

17/09/2026
As promised I am checking back. Interestingly, I feel like I've already adapted to this deficit. I don't really have food noise anymore and the potatoes are so filling that last night I had to look for a carb source because I had to consume 20 more grams of carbs for the day. When i was in university, I used to eat once a day because it helped with my focus and I am actually feeling like I can concentrate better. Chewing gum has been extremely useful today. 

19/09/2026
I've adjusted to the deficit and I really don't get hungry anymore. One interesting thing I've noticed, I am getting tired more easily and go to bed earlier at around 10:30-11pm. My dreams are becoming so extremely vivid but they're tied deeply to my emotions, particularly gratitude for some reason. Last night I had a dream where I was talking to an old friend and telling them how much I appreciate them and how much their actions meant to me. I had another dream where I was at the end of my cut and I was doing a lot of poses infront of a mirror. Before the cut I would sleep at perhaps 1am and I wouldn't dream at all. I do need to purchase some electrolytes though as I am starting to get a headache and my body is constantly flushing water. I'm going to just buy either liquid IV or a similar cheap solution and that will hopefully get rid of a lot of my problems.
